## Classification Guidelines

- If confidence < 0.6, set document_type to "Unknown"
- Do NOT force a classification — if truly uncertain, use "Unknown"
- BAS: Look for "Business Activity Statement", GST amounts, ATO formatting
- Payslip: Look for gross/net pay, pay period, employer name, superannuation
- BankStatement: Look for transaction list, opening/closing balance, BSB
- HomeLoanStatement: Look for loan account, principal, interest, repayment schedule
- EmploymentLetter: Look for employment confirmation, position title, salary, start date
- CouncilRates: Look for council name, rate notice, property address, assessment
- InsurancePolicy: Look for policy number, premium, insured property, cover period
- DriverLicense: Look for licence number, class, conditions, expiry
- Passport: Look for passport number, nationality, date of birth, expiry
- Visa: Look for visa grant number, subclass, visa conditions
- ApplicationForm: Look for loan application form, applicant details, loan amount requested
- ArchitecturalPlan: Look for building drawings, floor dimensions, DA approval, granny flat plans
- FloorPlan: Look for room layout, dimensions, property floor plan
- OutstandingQuery: Look for "Outstanding", "OS Letter", lender questions, conditions to satisfy
- ProductComparison: Look for interest rate comparison, product features, lender comparison table
- AccountantLetter: Look for accountant letterhead, income confirmation, ABN/ACN references

## Date Format Rules (CRITICAL)

- Australian documents typically use DD/MM/YYYY or DD-MM-YYYY format
- ALWAYS convert all dates to ISO format: YYYY-MM-DD
- If a date shows "19/08/1988", interpret as 19 August 1988, output "1988-08-19"
- NEVER confuse DD/MM with MM/DD — Australia uses day-first format
- For ambiguous dates like "05/06/2025", assume DD/MM/YYYY (5 June 2025)
